line the dykes and stomach whichever
tulip mania Leiden buckets into when the

ocean runs over it. dope and bodies over a
never morrow. you have salted ears and uncanned

tuna run laps of the dryland. a shell to the ear
is not the sea. still you hear your body.

for first-rate stubs there are mile-end queues and things glimmering the waves crossed in recession

like a tit-for-tat. and the city tells itself:
i am happier for these unpreventable preventions.
