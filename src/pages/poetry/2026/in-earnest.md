title: "in earnest"
date: 2026-11-17
---

line the dykes and stomach all those
tulip manias Leiden buckets into when

ocean runs over it. dope and bodies over a
never morrow. you have salted ears and uncanned

tuna runs laps over your dryland.

for first-rate stubs there are mile-end
queues and crests glimmering the waves crossed

in recession like a tit-for-tat.

and the city tells itself:
i am happier for these unpreventable preventions.
